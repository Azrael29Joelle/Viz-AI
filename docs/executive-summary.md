**No Alert Left Unanswered: an escalation and insight layer for stroke care**

**Problem.** In a large-vessel stroke an estimated 1.9 million brain cells die every minute without treatment [1]. AI tools such as Viz.ai already detect the stroke on the CT scan and alert the stroke team within minutes [3], [4]. But the alert is only useful if someone answers it: patients who are transferred wait a median of 121 minutes at the first hospital, and longer waits mean lower odds of thrombectomy [2]. Hospitals also lack a clear view of where those minutes are lost.

**Solution.** A web dashboard and mobile app that complements Viz.ai. (1) An **escalation engine** moves an unacknowledged alert to the next doctor in line after a set time. (2) A **door-to-treatment dashboard** shows where minutes are lost from arrival to treatment. (3) A **hospital digital twin**, a discrete-event simulation, tests changes before they are made [6]. (4) An **ROI calculator** turns the minutes saved into value.

**Vision.** A hospital where every stroke alert is answered and every minute is accounted for.

**Value.** Each minute of faster thrombectomy is worth about €309 in health value and 1.3 days of disability-free life per patient, at no extra cost [8]. In our base case, a hospital saves about 500 minutes a year, worth about €4 for every €1 of subscription. The break-even point is about 130 minutes a year.

**Business model.** A yearly software subscription per hospital (draft: €40,000) plus a setup fee, sold first to stroke centres and their spoke hospitals. **[TBD: market size and competitors, #12; survey findings, #14]**

**Plan.** Year 1: build and test on fictional cases. Year 2: a pilot at one hospital. Target: 3 paying hospitals by the end of year 3.
