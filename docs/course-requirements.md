# Course requirements: our summary

This is our own summary of the syllabus and lecture guidance. The original course files are on the course website; they are not stored here because this repo is public.

## Grading
| Part | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Final team presentation (English, every member presents a part) | 90% |
| Ongoing work: two midterm presentations (required, feedback only), class exercises, attendance | 10% |

The final deck must cover theoretical, marketing and financial aspects, plus a full bibliography. Submitted with it: a **half-page executive summary** and the **Excel files of the financial statements** with all the data and calculations.

## Timeline (by course week)
- **Weeks 3-5:** literature search (ScienceDirect via the JCE library, Google Scholar; Perplexity with sources, all checked). Write sections 2.1-2.6.
- **Week 5: Midterm 1** on the idea, the scientific/technological basis from literature, and the block algorithm. 4-5 polished slides, 5-7 min.
- **Weeks 6-7:** marketing literature, market survey, sections 3.1-3.7. **Midterm 2:** the full deck so far, 10-12 min, everyone speaks.
- **Week 8:** IP strategy, section 2.7.
- **Week 10:** Lean Canvas team meeting, executive summary. Final: max 15 min, 13-14 content slides, 3-5 min Q&A.
- **Week 12:** financial plan (4.1-4.6). The team leader fills in the final-presentation scheduling form.

## Lecture guidance
- **Technological feasibility check:**
  - (a) Ask an AI (Perplexity, GPT) to judge the idea only on technological feasibility, using only academic sources.
  - (b) Check it yourself on ScienceDirect: Review + Research articles, 2024-2026, "Export citation to text".
- **Integrative literature slide:** two sources; 1-2 sentences on what each contributes; one synthesis sentence linking them; IEEE citations [1], [2]; reference list. Synthesis, not just a summary.
- **Algorithm slides:** explain the technologies themselves, not just their names.
- **Pitch structure:** hook, pain point, solution, unique value proposition, market opportunity, team, call to action. Use emotion *and* numbers.
- **Design:**
  - Build your own master, not an Office theme.
  - Sans-serif font.
  - About 90% white or 90% black background.
  - At most 6 lines x 6 words; text at least 36 pt; titles at least 6 pt larger.
  - Pictures over text.
  - Put numbers in context ("dress up numbers").
  - Practice.
- **In-class pitch feedback criteria:** clarity and persuasion; technological innovation; initial technological feasibility; initial business feasibility (0-5 each).

## AI rules
- Using AI critically is **required**.
- Every use must be stated explicitly, with an appendix listing the tools and the exact prompts (screenshots).
- Work and presentations are checked for AI-generated content; undeclared use leads to a disciplinary committee.
- Never enter personal or confidential data into AI tools.
- The students are responsible for the accuracy of everything presented.
